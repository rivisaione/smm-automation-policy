SMM Automation

A private scheduling tool for YouTube channels we manage.

SMM Automation is an internal tool used by a small social media management team. When a video has been approved, the tool uploads it to the YouTube channel it belongs to. It fills in the title, description and tags, and schedules the video to go live at the planned date and time.

It is not a public service. Only channel owners who have given permission through Google's sign-in screen are connected, and each channel only receives its own videos.
