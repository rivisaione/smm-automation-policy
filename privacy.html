Privacy policy

SMM Automation · last updated 9 November 2026

This policy explains what SMM Automation (the "tool") does with Google and YouTube data. The tool is used privately by a social media management team to schedule videos to YouTube channels that have given it permission.

What the tool accesses

  • YouTube: the connected channel's name and ID, its list of uploaded and scheduled videos, and permission to upload videos, set thumbnails, add videos to playlists and change a video's privacy or schedule.
  • Google Drive: only the files in one "Social Media Manager" folder that is shared with the tool. It reads approved videos and moves them between sub-folders after upload.
  • Google Sheets: it reads an optional schedule sheet and adds rows to an upload log sheet in that same folder.

How the data is used

Data is used only to upload, schedule and check videos for the channel they belong to, and to send the team a notification about the result. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone else. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Google sign-in tokens are stored encrypted as private secrets in the team's private GitHub account and are used only by the tool's scheduled job. Video files are downloaded temporarily to process the upload and deleted straight afterwards.

Removing access and deleting data

A channel owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have stored tokens and logs deleted, email contactframeless2021@gmail.com. Requests are handled within 7 days.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
